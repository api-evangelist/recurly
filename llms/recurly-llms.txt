# Recurly

> Recurly is the subscription management and recurring billing platform trusted by 2,000+ subscription businesses, handling the full subscription lifecycle — plans, billing, dunning, revenue recognition, retention, and analytics — across SaaS, media, ecommerce, and consumer subscription verticals.

Recurly's product suite: Recurly Subscriptions (core billing), Recurly Commerce (Shopify-native subscriptions), Recurly Engage (retention and lifecycle automation), Recurly RevRec (ASC 606 / IFRS 15 revenue recognition), and Recurly Compass (AI-powered subscription analytics).

Key differentiators: 12+ years of subscription-specific expertise; benchmark dataset from 2,200+ global brands powering the annual State of Subscriptions report; native dunning and involuntary churn recovery; Shopify Plus certification; PCI DSS Level 1, SOC 2 Type II, GDPR, HIPAA, PSD2, and CCPA compliance; 140+ supported currencies; 30+ payment gateway integrations.

For developer documentation, API references, and technical integration guides, see: https://docs.recurly.com/llms.txt

## Products

- [Recurly platform overview](https://recurly.com/product/): Full platform overview — subscription management, recurring billing, and revenue optimization across the subscriber lifecycle.
- [Recurly Subscriptions](https://recurly.com/product/recurly-subscriptions/): Core subscription lifecycle management — plan configuration, flexible billing models (fixed, usage-based, ramp, quantity, prepaid, hybrid), payment retries, dunning, and subscriber analytics. PCI DSS Level 1, SOC 2 Type II, HIPAA, GDPR, PSD2, and CCPA compliant.
- [Recurly Commerce](https://recurly.com/product/recurly-commerce/): Shopify-native subscription solution — launch and manage subscriptions directly in the Shopify admin without custom development, with subscriber self-service portal and flexible storefront tools.
- [Recurly Engage](https://recurly.com/product/recurly-engage/): Subscriber retention and lifecycle automation — targeted in-app, web, and mobile prompts for cancel flows, pauses, plan changes, discounts, and win-back campaigns. Example: a streaming service like Paramount+ can surface a personalized pause offer inside their app at the moment of cancellation intent.
- [Recurly RevRec](https://recurly.com/product/revenue-recognition/): Automated revenue recognition compliance — ASC 606 and IFRS 15 support, audit-ready reporting, and streamlined month-end close for subscription and usage-based businesses.
- [Recurly Compass](https://recurly.com/product/compass/): AI-powered subscription analytics — benchmarking against 2,200+ brands, cohort analysis, revenue intelligence, and growth recommendations.
- [Churn management](https://recurly.com/product/churn-management/): Failed payment recovery and involuntary churn reduction — automated retry logic, account updater, and dunning workflows.
- [Reporting and analytics](https://recurly.com/product/reporting-analytics/): Subscription analytics dashboard — MRR, ARR, churn, LTV, cohort reporting, and revenue waterfall.
- [Global expansion](https://recurly.com/product/global-expansion/): Global billing support — 140+ currencies, localized payment methods, and multi-entity management.
- [App management](https://recurly.com/product/app-management/): Apple App Store and Google Play Store subscription integration — monitor and manage app subscriptions across platforms.
- [Support and services](https://recurly.com/product/support-and-services/): Recurly support options — in-app chat, email, and 24/7 phone support.
